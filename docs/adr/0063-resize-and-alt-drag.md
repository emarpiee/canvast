# ADR 0063: Figma-style resize, Alt-drag copies, Alt cycles the view, fit buttons

Status: accepted (owner request, 2026-10-16)

- **Handles:** the four corners and (for an upright box wide or tall enough) the middle of each side. A corner or a side of an **upright picture** (or a selection of only such pictures) is free: it follows the pointer in its axes, so the shape can change; a side changes one axis. Strokes, text, turned items and turned boxes keep their proportions whatever is held (a stroke's width and a text's glyphs cannot be stretched; ADR 0018, `scaleAbout`). A side handle on those scales uniformly about the opposite side's middle.
- **Modifiers while dragging a handle:** Shift keeps the proportions; Alt resizes about the middle of the box; Ctrl (or Cmd) turns snapping off. They can change during the drag.
- **Snapping:** it is the dragged *edge* that snaps, not the pointer (the edge sits where the pointer is less the offset it was grabbed at): the dragged edge (corner: both) snaps within 6 px to the left, middle or right (top, middle, bottom) of other visible objects, with the same guide lines as moving. With Shift on a corner only the nearer line holds and the other axis follows from the proportions. Snapping follows the "Snap and guides" setting.
- **Alt+drag the selection** starts the drag with a copy (placed exactly on the original, `duplicateSelection(…, 0)`) and moves the copy; the original stays. Alt+click (no drag) still cycles through overlapping items; Alt tapped alone does not fire when a pointer was pressed while it was held.
- **Alt tapped alone:** brings the selection into view; tapped again while the view is where it was left, it fits everything that counts as visible at this zoom, and a third tap goes back to the selection. "At this zoom" with a selection: the selected items plus every other legible item (4 px up to 16 windows) within a factor of 8 of the selection's size and at most 12 selection-sizes away (so it means the same however far the view has zoomed in; far or very differently sized items stay out, which keeps the fit from zooming out to nothing). With nothing selected: legible items within 3 window-widths of the window that are at most 4 windows large.
- **Fit buttons** beside zoom out and in (bottom right): *Fit the selection in view* (disabled with nothing selected) and *Fit everything in view*, for screens with no Alt key.
- **Not done:** free stretching of strokes and text; resize handles for turned boxes' sides.

**Resetting:** *Reset rotation* removes the turn of the selected items about each one's own middle (exact: turning by the opposite angle); *Reset proportions* gives an upright stretched picture its pixel aspect ratio back, keeping its width and its middle. Both are buttons in the selection bar, shown only when something selected can be reset, and one undo step each. Strokes and text are never stretched, so they have nothing to reset but their turn.
