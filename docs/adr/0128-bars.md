# ADR 0128 — Bars: one place and one row for each kind of control

- Status: accepted (v0.18.0)
- Supersedes: the placement of the selection bar in ADR 0051; the "more" menus of the options bar (ADR 0068 stays for rows and cards)
- Related: ADR 0096 (layers), ADR 0113/0126 (text look), ADR 0108 (sheets)

## Problem

Controls for the same job lived in different places (the options bar at the top, a floating bar under the selected item), the options bar wrapped into three rows when a text on a sheet was picked, and several things were hidden in "⋯" menus (Selection tool options, Initial shape, Arrange and align, Combine, the colours of a selection beyond six).

## Decision

Three zones, each with one job, each in a fixed place:

| Zone | Job | Place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rail | Choose a tool | Left, vertical |
| **Properties bar** (`ui/toolbar.ts`) | What the tool or the selection LOOKS like: colour, fill and outline, size, font, alignment, sheet size, corner radius, pin, crop and adjust, snap | Top centre, one row |
| **Actions bar** (`ui/context-bar.ts`) | What you DO to the selection: duplicate, group, tag, library and file actions, resets, text to path, the four shape operations, sheet and attach commands, align, distribute, grid and its spacing, order, lock, hide, delete | Bottom centre, above the readout, one row |
| Dock | History and panels | Top right |

- **Nothing is attached to an item.** The selection bar no longer floats under the picked item; it neither covers the picture nor jumps. Only what belongs to the item stays on it: handles, its name, a player.
- **One row, never a menu.** Every control that was in a menu is a button now: align, distribute, grid and the space between items; Union, Intersect, Subtract, Exclude, add to and take out of a boolean group, flatten, release; Set, Update and Reset initial shape; axis-aligned box, ignore the object that fills the view, Show all hidden, Unlock all; every colour of a selection has its chip. Modifiers of one thing stay popovers (outline options, the colour and gradient choosers, Adjust).
- **One kind at a time.** When the picked items are of different kinds, chips at the start of the Properties bar (Sheet, Text, Shape, Picture, Stroke) choose which kind's properties the row shows; the others are switched off (`data-off`), not moved. Each control says which kinds it is for (`data-kind`; `any` for all, `tool` for what shows with nothing picked).
- **When the row is wider than its room** it scrolls sideways and its ends fade (`data-scroll`). This is the one place the bars give way; it is never a menu. Labels were cut first (Colour, Font).
- **One component.** Every bar is a row of `.tb-group`s (`ui/bar.ts`, `syncGroups`): a group shows when a control in it shows; the line between groups is one CSS rule, never a separator element, so there is no leading line and no dangling one.
- **One meaning for "on".** A filled button is on (a tool, a switch, a choice); a ring only marks the colour or size chosen among swatches.

## Not done

Phone-width layout of the Actions bar (it scrolls); the selection bar's old placement around a media player's bar is gone, so a player's bar and the Actions bar can meet when the item is at the bottom of the view.
